LimitMark — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

LimitMark is a Windows desktop application that monitors the usage limits, API-key balances and spending of AI developer services you already use (such as Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and OpenRouter). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

  • LimitMark does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.
  • All of your settings, history and local analytics stay on your own device.
  • The developer operates no server that receives your data and runs no advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

LimitMark stores the following locally, in your Windows user profile, and never sends it to the developer:

  • Your application settings (theme, language, layout, notification thresholds).
  • A local history database of the usage, quota, balance and spend values it has observed.
  • Optional local analytics derived from Codex/Claude command-line tools installed on your machine.
  • Credentials you enter for a provider, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) for your user account. Where a provider offers its own command-line or IDE sign-in (for example Claude Code, Codex or Cursor), LimitMark reuses that existing credential store and does not copy, renew or modify it.

Network connections

To show your limits and balances, LimitMark connects directly from your device to the official API endpoints of the services you choose to monitor, using your own account or API key. These requests go to the respective providers, not to the developer. LimitMark reads usage, quota, balance and spending information; it does not run AI inference and does not perform account-administration actions without your explicit confirmation. Your use of each provider is also governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

Children

LimitMark is a developer tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not receive your data, and therefore does not share or sell it. LimitMark contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Your choices

You can clear the local history, disable local analytics, or remove any stored credential from within the application at any time. Uninstalling the application removes the program; your locally stored settings and history can be deleted from your user profile folder.
